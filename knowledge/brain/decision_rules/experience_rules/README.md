# Experience Rules (EX)

- **Family:** EX
- **Stage:** 4 -- Experience Perception
- **Source:** `docs/architecture/ADR-010-decision-rules-framework.md`

Rules in this folder govern **how a space is
experienced** by the people in it. They fire when the
Brain produces or consumes an `ExperienceProfile`.

V1 ships no rules. Future EX rules will target the
seven perception axes (scale proportion, spatial
relationship, atmosphere, story feeling, imagination,
stay desire, vitality) defined in
`knowledge/brain/experience_perception/README.md`.
