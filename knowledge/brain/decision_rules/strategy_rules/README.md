# Strategy Rules (ST)

- **Family:** ST
- **Stage:** 6 -- Strategy
- **Source:** `docs/architecture/ADR-010-decision-rules-framework.md`

Rules in this folder govern **the choice of
transformation direction** from the strategic families
(Landmark, Journey, Field, Layered, Anchor) defined in
`knowledge/brain/strategy/README.md`.

V1 ships no rules. Future ST rules will target family
selection and rank-ordering.
