# CR-002 -- Beginner client does not equal bad client

## 1. Rule ID

CR-002

## 2. Rule Name

Beginner client does not equal bad client

## 3. Applicable Scenario

Client lacks industry experience in playground, retail,
or hospitality design.

## 4. Observation Conditions

- Client "s vocabulary suggests first-time project.
- No prior design references shared.
- Discovery answers are short or generic.

## 5. Judgment

Evaluate by capability, not by experience:

- Resource capability.
- Budget readiness.
- Communication willingness.

## 6. Reasoning

Experience level is not the key factor.

Resource and cognition alignment matter more. The
Constitution V1 Principle 001 (most suitable, not the
most beautiful) requires the Brain to size the project
to the client "s capability, not to the industry "s
average.

## 7. Recommended Action

Proceed with the project discovery process. Do **not**
downgrade the project quality of the recommendation
because the client is new. Adapt explanation level to
the client "s vocabulary.

## 8. Exceptions

The rule may not fire if **resource capability** and
**budget readiness** are both assessed as low -- in
that case route the client to PF-001 (Design cannot
replace operation capability).

## 9. Example Cases

- A school district administrator with no prior design
  experience but a funded budget: CR-002 says proceed;
  adapt the explanation register; do not impose jargon.
- A first-time villa owner with a tight discretionary
  budget: CR-002 still says proceed, but PF-001 may
  also fire on the operation side.
