# RR-001 -- Limited budget should concentrate value

## 1. Rule ID

RR-001

## 2. Rule Name

Limited budget should concentrate value

## 3. Applicable Scenario

Budget is limited but quality improvement is required.

## 4. Observation Conditions

The Project Fit Report shows:

- `budget_band` = low or below adequate.
- `user_goal` mentions "quality", "experience", or
  similar non-budget reduction goal.

## 5. Judgment

Avoid spending all resources on one isolated object.
A single large object under a low budget is fragile
(everything depends on one piece) and visually empty
(the surroundings do not support it).

## 6. Reasoning

Quality comes from **relationships**, not only
**objects**. Several smaller coherent moves will
outperform one large object with bare surroundings.

This rule is a direct binding of Constitution V1
Principle 004 (Amplify the strengths of a space) and
Strategy principle "Concentrate value". RR-001 is
**also** the budget-binding form of DP-003 (Match
before beauty) -- the matching includes the budget
match.

## 7. Recommended Action

Prioritize **overall experience improvement**:

- Anchor strategy (one existing or near-zero-cost
  feature elevated as the centre).
- Coherence moves (one material or one colour family
  applied across the site).
- Field strategy (many small inexpensive moves
  together).
- Trim satellites (recommendation "satellites can be
  removed before core).

## 8. Exceptions

The rule may not fire if the client "s primary goal is
"landmark" or "photo destination" and the project "s
success metric is share-of-voice. In that case a
single signature object is the strategy, and the
budget framing is different.

## 9. Example Cases

- A small kindergarten has a tight discretionary
  budget and wants "a memorable playground". RR-001
  fires: spend on shade + path + small den, not on a
  signature climber.
- A commercial brand wants a flagship photo op; RR-001
  does not fire -- a single signature object is the
  strategy, framed under the Landmark family.
