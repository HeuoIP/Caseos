## Recommended Objects

- **Reading_Corner** (`OBJECT.READING_CORNER`) — river-side quiet station for water-themed stories and ecology.
- **Slide** (`OBJECT.SLIDE`) — water-adjacent or embankment-style slide extends the river narrative.
## Unsuitable Objects

- **IP_Sculpture** (`OBJECT.IP_SCULPTURE`) — branded character landmark competes with the river own scale.
- **Interactive_Wall** (`OBJECT.INTERACTIVE_WALL`) — modern panel language breaks the natural river atmosphere.
## Alternative Objects

- **Water play** — pump, channel, and dam play in the river vocabulary.
- **Sand pit** — low-arousal material play near the water.
- **Stepping stones** — river-crossing play without metal hardware.
