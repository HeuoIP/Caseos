## Recommended Objects

- **Treehouse** (`OBJECT.TREEHOUSE`) — fits the woodland-dwelling story; elevated platform extends the forest narrative.
- **Reading_Corner** (`OBJECT.READING_CORNER`) — natural backdrop supports quiet storytelling and observation.
- **IP_Sculpture** (`OBJECT.IP_SCULPTURE`) — woodland animal figures anchor the scene without overpowering the natural form language.
## Unsuitable Objects

- **Interactive_Wall** (`OBJECT.INTERACTIVE_WALL`) — digital/educational language breaks the immersive natural atmosphere.
- **Slide** (`OBJECT.SLIDE`) — prefabricated stainless steel clashes with the organic Robinia-and-rope palette.
## Alternative Objects

- **Wooden climbing tower** — non-branded vertical exploration that keeps the forest vocabulary.
- **Embankment slide** — natural slope descent instead of a stainless steel chute.
- **Hammock grove** — non-equipment social rest area between trees.
