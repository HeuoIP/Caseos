## Recommended Objects

- **Treehouse** (`OBJECT.TREEHOUSE`) — lookout tower reads as a knight watchtower.
- **IP_Sculpture** (`OBJECT.IP_SCULPTURE`) — knight figure or coat-of-arms sculpture anchors the chivalric narrative.
- **Slide** (`OBJECT.SLIDE`) — tournament or joust-arc slide extends the knight-quest story.
## Unsuitable Objects

- **Interactive_Wall** (`OBJECT.INTERACTIVE_WALL`) — modern educational panels break the medieval knight atmosphere.
- **Reading_Corner** (`OBJECT.READING_CORNER`) — quiet reading nook undercuts the knight-quest energy.
## Alternative Objects

- **Climbing wall** — non-IP vertical structure in the castle-stonewall vocabulary.
- **Rope bridge** — drawbridge or wall-walk crossing in the castle vocabulary.
